# Instructions

1. **Memorization**: You will see an image of a marine environment for a few seconds. Pay close attention to the details.
2. **Distractor**: A simple math problem will appear. Solve it and press Enter.
3. **Recall**: A white canvas will appear. Draw a rectangle where you remember seeing a specific object.
4. **Description**: Describe the scene and the object you identified.

Click **Next** to proceed.
